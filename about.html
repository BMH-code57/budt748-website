BMGT407 - INFORMATION SYSTEMS PROJECTS

BMGT407 is the capstone course for Information Systems majors. Students apply knowledge to develop real-world IS solutions for business clients. Through a complete development process, teams design and implement functional prototypes solving practical business challenges, preparing for careers in technology and consulting.
