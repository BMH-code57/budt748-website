Systems Projects

Bringing classroom knowledge into the real world.

BMGT407 is the capstone course for Information Systems majors. Students apply their knowledge to develop information systems solutions for business clients.

Through a complete development process, teams design and implement functional prototypes that address practical business challenges and prepare them for work in technology and consulting.
